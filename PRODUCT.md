# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
1. **Gift-Givers & Chocolate Lovers**: People seeking heartfelt, handcrafted, small-batch artisanal chocolates for loved ones, family tables, birthdays, holidays, and moments of personal indulgence.
2. **Corporate & Bespoke Event Gifters**: Clients seeking personalized, thoughtful chocolate boxes and custom packaging with authentic homemade character rather than generic commercial hampers.

## Product Purpose
OiAI ("Oops! I Ate It.") brings heartfelt, handmade chocolates to chocolate lovers and gifters. It celebrates spontaneous moments of sweetness, shared family tables, and the "happy little accidents" of small-batch chocolate crafting in Hyderabad, India. Success means connecting people through genuine warmth, delightful handmade recipes, transparent prices, honest customer taste notes, and memorable gifting experiences.

## Positioning
Handcrafted emotional warmth, small-batch kitchen intimacy, and playful authenticity ("Happy little accidents", "Tiny kitchen · Big feelings"). Unlike mass-manufactured commercial confectionery or aloof luxury chocolatiers, OiAI centers the human stories, nostalgic memories, and real community feedback behind every piece.

## Operating Context
- Responsive web storefront accessed on mobile and desktop browsers by gifters and dessert lovers.
- Studio admin dashboard (`/admin`) for product catalogue management, category organizing, reviewing customer feedback notes, and managing contact/sample settings.
- Direct customer interactions and orders handled via WhatsApp and Instagram.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Showcase catalog displaying handmade chocolate varieties with transparent pricing, weights, tasting notes, and flavor descriptions.
- Dynamic community feedback & taste note submission system with context-aware forms (chocolate taste review vs. memory/story sharing).
- Live community reviews section on the home page highlighting customer ratings and chocolate impressions.
- **Current Ordering State**: Showcase catalog, transparent pricing, and taste note/memory feedback are fully active; direct online payment gateways are intentionally disconnected/held offline in favor of direct inquiry and community taste notes.
- Configurable storefront contact settings (WhatsApp, Instagram, Email) and sample dropdown choices persisted in Supabase database.

## Brand Commitments
- **Name**: OiAI ("Oops! I Ate It.")
- **Taglines**: "Homemade chocolates. Happy little accidents." / "Tiny kitchen · Big feelings."
- **Location**: Made with love in Hyderabad, India.
- **Visual World**: Warm vintage paper tones, cocoa dust, nostalgic editorial typography, and hanging ribbon chocolate cards.
- **Tone of Voice**: Warm, intimate, playful, nostalgic, and unpretentious.

## Evidence on Hand
- Full collection of 9+ signature handmade chocolates (Secret Crush, Bad Habit, Sweet Surrender, Sugar Rush, Mixed Signals, First Love, Dark Affair, Pocket Mischief, Caught Snacking).
- Curated photography of handmade pralines, truffles, gift details, and cocoa shards in `/images/oiai/`.
- Live customer taste notes and feedback records in Supabase database.

## Product Principles
1. **Intimacy Over Industry**: Celebrate the charm, individuality, and warmth of a small handmade kitchen over cold factory uniformity.
2. **Every Bite Has a Story**: Chocolate is treated as a love language—shared around family tables, in late-night texts, or as small personal wins.
3. **Honest, Unhurried Discovery**: Let customers explore flavors, read genuine taste impressions, and connect directly without aggressive sales pressure.
4. **Thoughtful Craftsmanship**: From recipe creation to parcel unboxing, every touchpoint should feel like receiving a gift from a friend.
